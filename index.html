How it works

Five taps to a better closet

These are real screens from ModTok 1.0 on iPhone.

1Sort

Photograph your clothes and file them by category.

2Style

Swipe through pieces to build a complete look.

3Save

Keep outfits by occasion, ready when you are.

4Share

Post looks as stories and get opinions.

5Sell

List what you no longer wear and get paid.

+

Add a piece in seconds

Tap the pink + button. Take a photo, give it a name, choose its category. Done.

Sell

Turn what you don't wear into cash

Buy less, wear more. Pass your pieces on to someone who'll love them.

  1. Pick a piece from your closetIt's already photographed and sorted.
  2. Tap "List an Item" and set your priceShare it to your story to find a buyer faster.
  3. Get paidPayouts go straight to your account.

Secure payments by Stripe. Buying and selling is available in the U.S. for now, with Canada coming soon.
