<idea>
**Design Movement**: Editorial Fashion Magazine meets Digital Native

**Core Principles**:
1. Asymmetric, editorial grid layouts that feel like a high-fashion magazine spread
2. Warm cream/ivory backgrounds with coral-pink and deep black as the only accent colors
3. Bold typographic hierarchy using a display serif for headings and clean geometric sans for body
4. Phone mockup as the hero centerpiece, surrounded by floating UI card fragments

**Color Philosophy**:
- Background: #F5F0EB (warm cream — evokes luxury fashion editorials)
- Primary: #E8736A (coral rose — energetic, feminine, modern)
- Black: #1A1A1A (near-black — editorial authority)
- White: #FFFFFF (card surfaces)
- The warmth of cream prevents the site from feeling cold or clinical

**Layout Paradigm**:
- Diagonal split sections alternating image-left / text-right
- Feature cards in staggered masonry columns
- Full-bleed hero with phone mockup tilted at 8° angle
- Sticky horizontal scrolling feature showcase

**Signature Elements**:
1. Black pill-shaped labels (mirroring the app's category pills)
2. Coral underlines on key headings
3. Floating mini-card "screenshots" orbiting the phone mockup

**Interaction Philosophy**:
- Hover on feature cards reveals coral overlay with description
- Scroll-triggered entrance animations for each section
- Phone mockup rotates slightly on scroll (parallax)

**Animation**:
- Framer Motion fade-up on section entry (0.4s, ease-out)
- Staggered card reveals (0.1s delay per card)
- Coral accent line draws in on heading hover
- Smooth tab switching in the interactive demo section

**Typography System**:
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) — bold, editorial authority for H1/H2
- Body: DM Sans — geometric, clean, modern for paragraphs and UI
- Labels: DM Mono — technical credibility for code snippets and badges
