Common questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open your Profile, go to Settings, and choose Delete account. This removes your account and your closet. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

A problem with an order?
Email us with the item name and the date of the order, and we will help sort it out with the buyer or seller.
